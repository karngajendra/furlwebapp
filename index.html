FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Furl free? +

Yes — your first 100 saves are free, forever. Furl Pro unlocks unlimited saves with a small yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Do I need an account? +

No. Furl has no accounts and no sign-in. Your library lives on your device and is quietly backed up — to iCloud on iPhone and via Android's automatic backup on Android.

What can I save with Furl? +

Anything you can share: reels, videos, articles, products, podcasts, playlists and notes. Furl recognizes 50+ sources automatically — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, Pinterest, Spotify, X and many more — and files each save with its preview, title and source.

How does saving work? +

Tap Share in any app, then tap Save. Furl's share sheet appears right where you are — no app-switching, no losing your place in the feed. Pick a folder as you save, or let it land in your Inbox and sort later.

Is my data private? +

Yes. Furl is private by default: no account, no feed, and no tracking you around the web. What you save is stored on your device, and a private Hidden folder behind Face ID keeps some saves just for you. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Which platforms does Furl support? +

Furl is built for iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. It's launching soon on the App Store and Google Play — the buttons above will point to the listing the moment it's live.
